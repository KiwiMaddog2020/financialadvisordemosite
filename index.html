The approach

Most people don’t need more financial products. They need someone who sees the whole picture, explains it plainly, and stays with them as it changes.

Three principles

I II III

Clarity

Before any recommendation, you’ll see where you stand today: every account, policy and obligation in one view, written in plain English. Good decisions start with an honest map.

Discipline

Markets move and headlines shout. A sound plan is designed in calm and followed through the noise, with clear rules for when to act and, just as important, when not to.

Stewardship

A plan is never finished. We revisit it every year and whenever life shifts (a new child, a new business, a new chapter) so your money keeps working for what matters most.

What I help with

One plan, every part of your financial life.

  1. 01

    Retirement planning

    When you can retire, how much you can spend, and how to turn savings into a dependable income that lasts.

  2. 02

    Investment management

    Diversified portfolios built around your timeline and temperament, rebalanced with intent rather than emotion.

  3. 03

    Tax-aware strategy

    Account location, Roth conversions and withdrawal sequencing, coordinated with your tax professional so you keep more of what you earn.

  4. 04

    Risk & insurance

    Life, disability and long-term care, reviewed objectively, so the people who depend on you are protected without overpaying.

  5. 05

    Estate coordination

    Beneficiaries, trusts and legacy wishes, organised and kept current alongside your attorney.

  6. 06

    Family & education goals

    College savings, a first home, supporting parents. The milestones that matter, funded on purpose.

Working together

What happens after you reach out.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    45 minutes · No cost · No pressure

    We talk about where you are, what you want, and what keeps you up at night. You’ll leave with at least one useful idea, whether or not we work together.

  2. 02

    Your blueprint

    Two to three weeks

    I gather the details and return with a written plan covering cash flow, investments, taxes, protection and estate. It shows clear priorities and the reasoning behind each one.

  3. 03

    Stewardship

    Ongoing

    We put the plan to work, then meet regularly to keep it current. When life changes, you have one call to make, and someone who already knows the whole story.
